# Terminology Ledger

Maintain recurring terms across the project:

| Source term | Preferred Chinese | Preferred English | Abbreviation | First definition | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:

- Preserve gene, protein, model, dataset, metric, theory, construct, and instrument names.
- Expand abbreviations on first use in each standalone artifact.
- Do not silently alternate translations.
- Use the ledger for manuscripts, figures, captions, slides, reviewer responses, and Data Availability statements.
- Mark uncertain translations `待核实`.
