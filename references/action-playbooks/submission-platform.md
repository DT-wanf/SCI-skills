# Submission Platform and Final External Actions

Use when the user must complete journal selection, account-based submission, file upload, metadata entry, author confirmation, payment, or final approval.

## Boundary

The agent may prepare and audit files but must not impersonate the author, accept legal terms, confirm authorship, submit without authorization, or invent journal requirements.

## Preparation

Verify the current official author instructions and collect:

- exact journal and article type;
- manuscript;
- figures and tables;
- supplementary files;
- cover letter;
- title page;
- author names, affiliations, ORCID, contributions, and corresponding author;
- funding, conflicts, ethics, consent, data/code statements;
- suggested or opposed reviewers;
- required checklists.

## Step-by-step submission guide

When current web access is available, verify the official current submission route before naming buttons.

Then guide the user:

1. Open the journal's official submission page.
2. Sign in or create the author's account.
3. Start the correct article type.
4. Enter title, abstract, keywords, and classifications.
5. Add authors in the approved order and verify affiliations.
6. Upload files in the required categories and order.
7. Enter declarations and data/code links.
8. Add reviewer information if required.
9. Generate and inspect the compiled proof.
10. Compare the proof against the local final checklist.
11. Resolve warnings.
12. The user performs the final legal confirmations and submission.

## Completion evidence

Ask the user to retain:

- submission ID;
- submitted file list and versions;
- compiled proof;
- confirmation email;
- unresolved warnings.

Do not request passwords or unnecessary account screenshots.

## Revision

For a revision, preserve comment IDs, response files, clean and marked manuscripts, change locations, and the editor's requested deadline.
