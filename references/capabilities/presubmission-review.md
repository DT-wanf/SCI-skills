# Pre-submission Review

## Use for

Mock peer review, novelty and rigor assessment, reviewer-risk diagnosis, or a final gate before submission.

## Build the fact base

Before reviewing, extract:

- paper type and target;
- central claim;
- claimed contribution;
- data and methods;
- key figures and results;
- limitations stated by the authors;
- missing sections or inaccessible supplements.

Ground concerns in manuscript locations. Do not invent absent experiments or facts.

## Three reviewer lenses

1. **Contribution reviewer** — originality, importance, positioning, broad relevance.
2. **Methods reviewer** — design, data, statistics, reproducibility, robustness.
3. **Communication reviewer** — argument, figures, terminology, transparency, accessibility.

## Output

For each reviewer provide:

- summary;
- major concerns;
- minor concerns;
- evidence location;
- why the issue matters;
- minimum repair;
- severity.

Then provide a cross-review synthesis:

- shared blocking issues;
- disagreements;
- highest-risk claims;
- prioritized revision plan;
- readiness: ready / conditionally ready / not ready.

Do not impersonate a named reviewer or predict an actual editor decision.
