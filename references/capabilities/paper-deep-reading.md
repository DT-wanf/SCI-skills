# Paper Deep Reading

## Use for

One-paper analysis, evidence cards, method reconstruction, experiment-to-claim mapping, limitations, comparison, and extracting reusable research ideas.

## Source boundary

State whether the source is:

- full paper with figures and tables;
- abstract only;
- publisher page;
- preprint;
- pasted excerpts;
- user notes.

Do not pretend abstract-only access supports full-method or full-limitations analysis.

## Reading depth

Choose one:

- `快速卡` — positioning, question, method, main finding, relevance;
- `标准精读卡` — complete evidence chain and limitations;
- `深度研究卡` — module logic, formulas, experiment matrix, conclusion boundaries, connections, and testable ideas.

Default beginners to `标准精读卡` unless they request otherwise.

## Standard card

1. Bibliographic position
2. Research question
3. Background and pain point
4. Core insight
5. Method overview
6. Module or mechanism logic
7. Data, cohort, materials, or benchmark
8. Experimental design
9. Metrics and baselines
10. Key results
11. Experiment-to-claim evidence table
12. Conclusion boundary
13. Author-stated limitations
14. Critical assessment
15. What can be learned or reused
16. Testable follow-up ideas

## Evidence labels

Use:

- `原文事实`
- `外部核验`
- `AI分析`
- `研究假设`

Attach page, section, figure, or table locators when available.

## Research ideas

For each idea state:

- unresolved problem;
- change from the source paper;
- required data or experiment;
- falsifiable result;
- minimum viable version;
- main risk.

Do not present an idea as novel without a broader verified search.
