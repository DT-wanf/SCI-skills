# Paper Presentation

## Use for

Journal club, group meeting, thesis seminar, defense, conference, or paper-sharing PPTX.

## Story first

Classify the source as methods, discovery, clinical, resource, materials, review, or mixed. Build an evidence-led arc:

1. Why the problem matters
2. What gap remains
3. What the paper did
4. How the method or study works
5. Which results support each claim
6. What is genuinely new
7. What remains uncertain
8. What the audience should remember

## Figure use

- Prefer source figures that carry evidence.
- Preserve captions and source locators.
- Crop without cutting labels, scale bars, legends, or statistical marks.
- Do not enlarge low-resolution screenshots beyond readability.
- Recreate a diagram only when source attribution and meaning remain clear.

## Default presentation profile

- Chinese explanatory text
- Preserve English scientific labels in figures
- Chinese font: Microsoft YaHei
- English font: Times New Roman
- Include speaker notes for interpretation and transitions

## Deliverable

Create the actual deck when presentation tools are available. Include:

- title and source;
- sectioned evidence story;
- key figures and captions;
- critical assessment;
- take-home messages;
- references;
- speaker notes.

## QA

Check overflow, alignment, crop completeness, font size, contrast, terminology, source attribution, slide density, and whether each slide has one clear purpose.
