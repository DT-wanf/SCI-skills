# Academic Polishing

## Use for

Language polishing, Chinese-to-English translation, restructuring, paragraph flow, title refinement, and journal-aware academic expression.

## Diagnose first

Classify issues:

- scientific logic;
- evidence or claim strength;
- structure and paragraph function;
- terminology consistency;
- grammar and style;
- formatting or LaTeX layout.

Do not use language improvement to conceal a scientific or evidence problem.

## Preserve meaning

- Keep numbers, units, genes, models, datasets, citations, and statistical notation unchanged unless correcting a verified error.
- Preserve hedging and causal strength.
- Do not add claims, literature, results, or novelty.
- Mark unclear source meaning as `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED`.

## Polish levels

- `轻度` — grammar, punctuation, clarity;
- `标准` — sentence structure, cohesion, academic diction;
- `深度` — paragraph order and argument reconstruction without changing evidence;
- `中译英` — publication-ready English with terminology ledger.

## Output modes

Provide one of:

- clean revised text;
- original/revised/reason table;
- revised text plus key scientific risks;
- bilingual version when requested.

## Final checks

Verify terminology, abbreviations, tense, voice, number agreement, citations, figure/table references, and claim strength.
