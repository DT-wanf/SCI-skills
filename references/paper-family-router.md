# Paper Family Router

## Core principle

Classify by the paper's main intellectual contribution.

| Code | Family | Primary question | Typical evidence | Main contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | Empirical | Does a relationship, mechanism, difference, intervention, or effect hold? | Dataset, sample, experiment, interview, observation, archival records | Evidence-based finding or mechanism test |
| I | Interpretive | What does a text, work, event, historical document, or cultural phenomenon mean, and how is that meaning produced? | Primary text, close reading, version, archive, historical context | Evidence-backed interpretation |
| M | Method/Application | How can a real problem be solved better with a method, model, system, process, or application? | Prototype, algorithm, process, benchmark, test, case, user evaluation | New or improved solution and validated utility |
| T | Theoretical | Which concept, proposition, theoretical dispute, or explanatory limitation requires clarification, correction, integration, or reconstruction? | Original theory texts, conceptual analysis, logical argument, comparison | Defensible theoretical position or framework |
| R | Review | What is known, how is the field structured, where do studies disagree, and what remains unresolved? | Transparent literature search, coding matrix, synthesis, knowledge map | Structured synthesis and research agenda |

## Hybrid routing

- Data plus a new model: usually `M` primary, `E` secondary, when the main claim is that the method improves performance.
- Data used to test a relationship or mechanism: `E` primary.
- Interviews used to interpret meaning: choose `I` when the contribution is interpretive; choose `E` when the interviews are systematically analyzed to answer empirical questions.
- Review plus proposed framework: choose `R` when the framework follows from systematic synthesis; choose `T` when the paper's main work is an original theoretical argument.

## Minimum diagnostic questions

Ask in Chinese only when not already answered:

```text
研究方向或对象：
你最希望论文最终证明、解释、提出或总结什么：
目前可以使用的数据、文本、文献、实验、代码或案例：
你是否准备提出新方法、模型或方案：
导师、课程、学校或目标期刊的要求：
```

## Representative-paper comparison

When the user is uncertain, use real papers to make the five forms concrete. Search and verify the paper title, authors, year, source, and relevance. Compare the research question, evidence, method, contribution, resource threshold, and fit. Label relevance as `精确同方向`, `同领域近似`, or `仅作类型示例`.
